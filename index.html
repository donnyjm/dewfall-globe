THE EVIDENCE BEHIND THE ATLAS

Explore with context.

DEWFALL has not yet produced validated, measured yield data. This atlas screens possibilities; it cannot establish a location’s actual output or suitability.

Climate and yield

The E1 screening scenario assumes 400 W cooling, a 5°C coil and sea-level pressure. This is not the calibrated digital twin. City inputs are approximate curated climate bins; many community and market inputs are rough latitude-based proxies. “Summer” and “Winter” follow the source dataset’s seasonal conventions, which are not consistently aligned between hemispheres. These are not live weather or exact-site forecasts.

The displayed range varies cooling from 300–500 W. It is a sensitivity range, not a confidence interval. No unvalidated sorbent or TEC contribution is credited. A geometric mean combines a heuristic need score with modeled yield for the fit ranking. Commercial-market scores use a separate demand proxy.

Places and water need

Land records come from NRCan Aboriginal Lands of Canada centroids. Canadian need layers carry dated ISC and FNHA snapshots from August–September 2026. World sites retain the original curated citations and approximate community coordinates. A marker does not establish a current advisory, community consent or willingness to buy. Check each linked source and engage through a Nation-led process.

Solar, population and funding

Solar is a latitude-based irradiance approximation with a 0.6 kWp array and 0.75 performance ratio assumption. Population and income are estimates or regional proxies. Funders are research starting points; eligibility, application windows and support have not been verified.

Earth and source files

NASA Blue Marble imagery and Earth night/ocean textures distributed with three-globe. Day/night illumination is illustrative, not a real-time solar position. Source records were imported on 4 September 2026.
