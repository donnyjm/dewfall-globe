## Coordinates method
- Named place in a source → Nominatim/OSM geocode of that place name (community centroid).
- No invented villages. Approximate chapter/settlement points only.
- Same place under two names collapsed (e.g. Wugularr = Beswick).

## Gaps — what we could **not** find as open lists
1. **No global open API** of Indigenous boil-water advisories analogous to Canada ISC LTDWA.
2. **DigDeep / Navajo Safe Water** interactive home-level maps exist but are not a clean public download of community centroids with licenses for republication; used named project towns/chapters instead.
3. **EPA SDWIS tribal dashboard** is system-level (PWS IDs), not a single “active tribal BWA” open CSV; pulled documented emergency cases instead.
4. **Australia**: no single national remote-Indigenous BWA feed; NT Power & Water annual report + WA Water Corp / Auditor-General / ABC case reporting used. Torres Strait named active BWA points were sparse in open search for 2020+.
5. **New Zealand**: Taumata Arowai does not publish a simple open Māori-community BWA map; Rūātoki is a documented local case. Many marae supplies are small/unregistered — no lat/lng list.
6. **Sápmi (Sámi)**: literature emphasizes hydropower / land-rights impacts more than domestic “no piped drinking water” settlements; **skipped** rather than invent domestic BWA points.
7. **Canada territories / Inuit**: ISC LTDWA layer already covers First Nations public systems; Nunavut/Nunavik haul-water communities are documented regionally but were not duplicated here to avoid overlap with the Canada globe layer.
8. **Latin America Andes (Quechua/Aymara)**: strong regional WASH disparity stats, but fewer citable settlement-level drinking-water failure URLs with stable names than Amazon mercury/oil cases — thin on purpose.
9. **Africa**: San/Maasai/Himba included only where a named place + URL existed; no continent-wide Indigenous BWA list.
10. **Pacific outer islands**: project documents often name “outer atolls” generically without a public village coordinate table.

## Confidence legend (internal; not in JS schema)
- **high** — official advisory, agency unserved list, or utility annual report naming the community
- **medium** — reputable NGO / peer-reviewed / major news naming the community with clear water-access claim
- **low** — regional estimate only → **excluded**
